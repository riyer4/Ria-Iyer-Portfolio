Love Letter Mod

DateOct 2023
ToolsTabletop mod
TeamRia Iyer + classmates
TabletopGame Mod

A mod of the existing game Love Letter with the romantic framing removed in favor of more assertive dynamics, restructured as a group of three players versus the princess (four players total).

The project explored how far a game's tone can shift while keeping its underlying systems intact.
