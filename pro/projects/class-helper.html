Class Helper

DateMar 2026
ToolsFigma
TeamRia Iyer
UI/UXHCI ResearchFigma

A prototype built for an HCI research course, where students use the site to build their class schedules by cross-referencing student input and professor ratings.

The site also includes study tips from previous students, difficulty graphs based on weeks in the quarter, and the option to submit new classes and ratings.
