Survive College

DateMar 2024
ToolsPixel-art puzzle game
TeamRia Iyer, Eric Lee, Edward Oh
Pixel ArtPuzzleTeam

A three-level pixel-art puzzle game reflecting the struggles of a college student trying to pass finals: their dorm, the journey to the bus stop, and finally their own brain.

The game is timed against an energy meter, the longer you take, the less energy remains. Level one is about gathering essentials before leaving the dorm; level two is dodging traffic en route to the bus; the final level is a maze representing the search for the right content to pass the final. Finishing awards a diploma stamped with remaining time.
