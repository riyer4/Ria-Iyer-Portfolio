Not My Job!

DateNov 2023
ToolsCard game
TeamRia Iyer
Card GameSolo Design

A card game illustrating roommate dynamics through chores and the excuses used to avoid them. A chore is presented, and each player has to make a convincing excuse to avoid doing it.

Each chore and excuse carries a time limit; whoever has the lowest excuse time takes the chore. The goal is to accumulate as little chore-time as possible.
