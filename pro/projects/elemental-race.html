Elemental Race

DateDec 2023
ToolsPhysical board game
TeamRia Iyer, Megan Amen-Kroeger, Hilda Mulungi, Jakob Pallock, Juan Garcia, Alexander Boria
Board GameTabletopTeam

A competitive 4-player board game where players explore the map, discover new levels, and sabotage or get sabotaged. The game runs on 9 decks: four movement decks (one per realm), four element-specific decks (one per realm), and one shared sabotage deck.

Landing on a star tile lets a player draw from their element deck to sabotage multiple opponents at once. As play progresses, the board physically opens to reveal each of three levels until it's fully expanded.
