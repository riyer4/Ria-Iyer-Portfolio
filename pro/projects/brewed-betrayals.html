Brewed Betrayals

DateOct 2023
ToolsTabletop game
TeamRia Iyer + classmates
TabletopSocial Deduction

A cooperative potion-brewing game where the group wins by completing potions together, except one or two players are secretly spies working to sabotage the brew. Colored cubes served as ingredients, drawn against recipes from a shared deck.

Nine potions exist in total; the group must successfully complete five to win.
