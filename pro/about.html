About

A little more about the person behind the work.

RI

My name is Ria Iyer. I'm fresh out of college with two degrees: a B.A. in Art: Games & Playable Media (AGPM) and a B.S. in Computer Science: Game Design (CSGD), with honors in computer science. I'm passionate about games, design, and building things people actually want to use.

This portfolio started as a high school assignment to showcase what I'd learned in a four-year graphic design program. I've since used it to track my work through college, moving from graphic design into game design and, most recently, into UI/UX and product work.

I like bringing a real point of view into my work. Every project here was made with a specific purpose behind it. I hope you enjoy looking around, and please feel free to reach out with any questions!
