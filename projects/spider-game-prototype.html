Spider Game Prototype

DATE 12/04/2025
TOOLS Phaser (JavaScript)
TEAM Ria Iyer + Team
Phaser 8-bit Team

This is a prototype for an 8-bit arcade style game that combines basic moving and eating mechanics with a lottery of mini games that depict the life cycle of a spider that gets slower and slower as time goes on and as the web grows larger.
