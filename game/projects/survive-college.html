Survive College

DATE 03/17/2024
TOOLS Pixel-art puzzle game
TEAM Ria Iyer, Eric Lee, Edward Oh
Pixel Art Puzzle Team

This game is a pixel-art puzzle game that reflects the struggles of a college student trying to pass their finals. The game has three levels: their dorm, the journey to the bus stop, and finally, their brain.

The game is timed — the more time you use, the less energy you have remaining. Run out of energy and you lose. Level one has players grabbing essentials before leaving the dorm; level two is dodging traffic on the way to the bus; the final level is a maze inside their own brain, searching for the right content to pass the final. Finish, and you get a diploma with your remaining time on it.
