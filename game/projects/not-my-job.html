Not My Job!

DATE 11/05/2023
TOOLS Card game
TEAM Ria Iyer
Card Game Solo Design

This game illustrates roommate relationships and issues through chores and how some roommates get out of them. A chore is presented, and your job is to make a good excuse to avoid doing it.

Each chore and excuse has a time limit; the person with the lowest excuse time takes the chore. The goal is to rack up the least amount of chore-time possible.
