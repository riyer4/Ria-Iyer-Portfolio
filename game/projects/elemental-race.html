Elemental Race

DATE 12/08/2023
TOOLS Physical board game
TEAM Ria Iyer, Megan Amen-Kroeger, Hilda Mulungi, Jakob Pallock, Juan Garcia, Alexander Boria
Board Game Tabletop Team

A competitive 4-person board game where players explore the map, discover new levels, and sabotage or be sabotaged. There are 9 decks: 4 movement decks (1 per realm), 4 element-specific decks (1 per realm), and 1 communal sabotage deck.

Land on the stars on your map and you can draw from your element-specific deck to sabotage multiple players at once. As players progress, the board physically opens to reveal the next level (3 in total) until it fully expands.
