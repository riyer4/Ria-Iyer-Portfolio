100's or Death

DATE 02/12/2025
TOOLS Phaser (JavaScript)
TEAM Ria Iyer
Phaser Endless Runner Solo

This game is an endless runner dodging and collecting game that involves the player collecting 100’s and avoiding getting hit by slippers — in other words, the player keeps getting good grades to appeal to their parents.

I wanted to reflect the basic household stereotype as a more light-hearted game that some people would be able to relate to.
