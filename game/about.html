PLAYER BIO

My name is Ria Iyer, I am 22 years old, and I am passionate about games, art, and music! I am currently approaching the end of my senior year in college as a Game Design and Computer Science double major.

This website was initially created as an assignment in high school to showcase everything I'd learned in a 4-year graphic design program. I now use it to track my projects through college, pivoting from graphic design to game design to UI/UX.

I love including aspects of my life in all of my work, conveying a story I once lived, breathed, and struggled through. The pieces here were each created with a special meaning and purpose behind them — I hope you enjoy looking around, and please feel free to reach out with any questions. Thank you!
